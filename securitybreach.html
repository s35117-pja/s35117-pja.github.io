Mega Pizzaplex System Overview

The Mega Pizzaplex operates as a fully integrated entertainment complex, combining performance zones, security systems and underground maintenance networks. Despite its bright and colorful appearance, the facility hides multiple system failures and corrupted security protocols.

GREGORY

STATUS: ACTIVE

A young boy trapped inside the Mega Pizzaplex. Throughout the night, Gregory must avoid hostile animatronics while uncovering the secrets hidden beneath the facility.

GLAMROCK FREDDY

STATUS: FRIENDLY

Unlike the other animatronics, Glamrock Freddy helps Gregory survive. He acts as a guide and protector throughout the events of Security Breach.

VANNY

STATUS: THREAT DETECTED

A mysterious follower of Glitchtrap. Vanny secretly manipulates events within the Pizzaplex and serves as one of Gregory's greatest threats.

Incident Report

During the Security Breach incident, multiple system failures occurred simultaneously. AI behavior anomalies, corrupted surveillance data, and unauthorized access led to complete loss of control over several sectors of the Pizzaplex.

The cause of these events remains partially unknown, but evidence suggests external interference within the central security network.
